# SEVEN ROOTS Packaging Specifications

**Document status:** Proposed production brief - physical sample and converter validation required  
**Brand:** SEVEN ROOTS African Botanical Oral Care  
**Positioning:** Ancient roots. Modern ritual.

## Shared product datum

- Target chewing-stick length: **165 mm** (6.50 in)
- Natural stick diameter: **10-14 mm** (0.39-0.55 in)
- Natural tolerance: **±10 mm length and ±3 mm diameter**
- Individual primary wrap: **180 × 28 mm flat**, food-contact-approved cellulose barrier pouch
- Primary wrap must carry the botanical name, plant part, origin, lot, packing date, best-before date, and storage instruction.
- Microperforation, moisture barrier, oxygen barrier, and seal strength must be chosen only after microbial and shelf-life testing.

## SR-T01 - Travel Sleeve

**Finished outside dimensions:** **190 × 38 × 22 mm** (7.48 × 1.50 × 0.87 in)  
**Pack count:** 1 wrapped stick  
**Primary role:** Trial, travel, hospitality, sampling, and subscription inserts

The Travel Sleeve is the smallest entry format. One individually wrapped chewing stick rests in a narrow kraft cradle inside a tamper-evident forest-green folding sleeve. Preparation and storage instructions should print on the inside flap or a compact folded strip so the package stays lightweight.

### Proposed construction

- 350-400 gsm uncoated folding paperboard outer sleeve
- Kraft-fibre retaining cradle
- One sealed cellulose primary wrap
- Terracotta format band and antique-gold brand detail
- Water-based matte protective coating where rub testing requires it

## SR-R05 - Daily Ritual

**Finished outside dimensions:** **200 × 110 × 38 mm** (7.87 × 4.33 × 1.50 in)  
**Pack count:** 5 wrapped sticks  
**Primary role:** Signature daily-use and gifting format

The Daily Ritual uses a pull-drawer structure with five individually wrapped sticks organized in one presentation row. A dedicated side or lower channel carries a reusable ventilated travel tube, while the ritual guide explains preparation, brushing, rinsing, air-drying, replacement, and cautions. A textile or paper pull tab provides a controlled reveal.

### Proposed construction

- 400 gsm forest-green outer sleeve
- 500-600 gsm kraft folding drawer or validated moulded-fibre tray
- Five sealed cellulose primary wraps
- Reusable vented travel tube: **180 mm long × 24 mm outside diameter**
- Folded ritual guide: finished **85 × 75 mm**
- Terracotta identity band and antique-gold brand detail

## SR-F12 - Family Reserve

**Finished outside dimensions:** **210 × 155 × 50 mm** (8.27 × 6.10 × 1.97 in)  
**Pack count:** 12 wrapped sticks  
**Primary role:** Household reserve, repeat purchase, and careful stock rotation

The Family Reserve holds twelve individually wrapped sticks in two indexed rows of six. A rigid folding carton and removable fibre tray protect the natural product in transit. The recloseable terracotta band provides tamper evidence at first opening and a clear visual cue for closing and storage after opening.

### Proposed construction

- 400-450 gsm uncoated folding paperboard outer carton
- Removable moulded-fibre or folded-kraft tray with two rows of six
- Twelve sealed cellulose primary wraps
- Recloseable paperboard identity band
- Ritual and care guide: finished **180 × 120 mm**, folded as required

## Brand application

- Forest green: `#173D32`
- Antique gold: `#D6AE62`
- Terracotta: `#C86C3A`
- Warm ivory: `#F2E8D8`
- Muted botanical gray: `#5A5D51`
- Front hierarchy: identity mark, SEVEN ROOTS wordmark, format name, count, and positioning line
- Side/back hierarchy: authenticated botanical name, origin, lot, use, care, storage, warnings, responsible-business details, barcode, and disposal guidance
- Decorative linework must be original and must not copy sacred, restricted, or community-owned symbols.

## Production validation gates

1. Condition representative sticks and confirm the 95th-percentile product envelope.
2. Create converter dielines using finished dimensions, selected board caliper, glue flap, grain direction, and machine tolerances.
3. Conduct fit, drop, vibration, compression, rub, seal-strength, migration, microbial, moisture, and accelerated shelf-life tests.
4. Validate packaging claims, recycling instructions, country-of-origin wording, and oral-care product claims for every destination market.
5. Approve a printed white sample and packed golden sample before mass production.

These measurements are a design and quoting basis, not a final manufacturing dieline.
